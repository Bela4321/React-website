ClusterSearch · my bachelor thesis

Search results are long lists; I wanted to see the topics first. ClusterSearch asks four academic databases at once, turns every abstract into a vector with a transformer model and lets HDBSCAN group them, with a map, a cluster tree and a list to explore the result. Ask it a full question.

Sudoku solver

Written down as a matrix, the rules of sudoku turn into an exact-cover problem. I found that encoding so elegant that I wanted to see it work: a short search now solves any puzzle in milliseconds.
