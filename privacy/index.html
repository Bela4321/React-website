Privacy notice

What happens with your data on belaschinke.com, in plain words. Last updated November 2026.

Who is responsible

Bela Schinke is responsible for this website (the "controller" under the GDPR). You can reach me at bela.schinke@gmail.com.

The short version

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking, shows no ads and loads nothing from other companies' servers: the font is hosted here. The sudoku solver runs entirely in your browser.

Hosting

The site and the game server run on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). To deliver a page, Cloudflare necessarily processes your IP address, the address of the page, the time, and technical details your browser sends, such as its user agent. Cloudflare uses this to deliver the site and protect it from attacks, and keeps short-lived logs. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare processes this data for me under a data processing agreement and may process it in the USA; Cloudflare is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Tic-tac-toe

When you join a room, the nickname you choose and a random player number are sent to the game server so that two players can find each other and a reload keeps your seat. If you open a public room, your nickname is shown to other visitors in the list of open rooms. A room and everything in it is deleted at the latest 10 minutes after the last player leaves. Legal basis: providing the game you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Your browser remembers your nickname (local storage) and your player number for the open tab (session storage). This is strictly necessary for the game you asked to use (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG), so it needs no consent. You can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

Email

If you email me, I use your address and message only to answer you, and delete them when the conversation is over unless the law requires me to keep them. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR.

Write to the email address above. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one where you live.
